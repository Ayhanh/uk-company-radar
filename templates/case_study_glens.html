Glens of Antrim Potatoes Enters Administration: What Creditors and Suppliers Need to Know

October 2026 · Companies House Gazette & Insolvency Overview

Following recent Companies House notices, the prominent Northern Ireland agri-food supplier Glens of Antrim Potatoes Ltd has officially entered administration. For hundreds of farmers, packaging vendors, logistics providers, and trade creditors across the UK, this marks a critical phase in managing trade receivables.

1. What Does "In Administration" Mean for Suppliers?

When a UK company enters administration, an administrative moratorium immediately takes effect. Unsecured creditors cannot take independent legal action or petition for winding up without the administrator's or court's permission. Administrators are appointed to either rescue the company as a going concern or achieve a better result for creditors than immediate liquidation.

2. Immediate Steps for Creditors:

  • Collate All Unpaid Invoices: Assemble all purchase orders, delivery notes, and outstanding account statements.
  • Check Retention of Title (RoT) Clauses: If your contract includes a valid RoT clause, notify the appointed administrators immediately before goods are processed or sold.
  • Submit Proof of Debt: Register your claim formally with the appointed joint administrators once contact details are published in The Gazette.

3. Why Real-Time Registry Sync Matters

In supply chain insolvencies, notices often appear in official regulatory registries days before public statements or press reports circulate. Tracking key accounts allows finance departments to halt extended credit limits before exposure compounds.
